About the studio

Makgolli Studio is a one-person studio — a side project for an amateur developer working on this outside a day job, named after 막걸리 (makgeolli), Korean rice wine and a personal favorite.

The current focus is KKORI, a bilingual divination app, with a KKORI sticker pack in preparation. What comes next isn't fixed to one category — projects here get built as ideas arrive, not to a roadmap.
