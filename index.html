About the studio

Makgolli Studio is a one-person creative studio offering original projects and language services. Its name comes from 막걸리 (makgeolli), Korean rice wine and a personal favorite.

Original work includes KKORI, a character appearing in LINE sticker packs and the studio's first Android app. Future projects may explore other ideas and characters.

The studio's own projects are designed bilingual from the start, for readers moving between Traditional Chinese and Korean.
