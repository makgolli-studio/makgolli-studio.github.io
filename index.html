About the studio

Makgolli Studio is a one-person studio — a side project for an amateur creator working on this outside a day job, named after 막걸리 (makgeolli), Korean rice wine and a personal favorite.

The current focus is KKORI, an original character of the studio's own design. The studio's first app, KKORI — Daily Fortune & Divination, is available on Google Play. KKORI sticker packs are available through the studio's LINE STORE page, while future apps and side projects may feature other ideas or characters.

So far, everything made here has been designed bilingual from the start, for readers moving between Traditional Chinese and Korean, rather than translated on as an afterthought.
